## Part 2: Throughout the Day  
### Interrupting Autopilot — Breaking Unconscious Patterns

**Purpose:**  
Insight without interruption does not produce change.  
You must **break unconscious patterns in real time**.

---

### Setup Instructions
- Create reminders or calendar events **now**
- Include the **question text** in each reminder
- Make them **random and non-conflicting**
- Best done while commuting, walking, or lying around

---

### Scheduled Pattern Interrupts

**11:00am**
```

What am I avoiding right now by doing what I’m doing?

```

**1:30pm**
```

If someone filmed the last two hours, what would they conclude I want from my life?

```

**3:15pm**
```

Am I moving toward the life I hate or the life I want?

```

**5:00pm**
```

What’s the most important thing I’m pretending isn’t important?

```

**7:30pm**
```

What did I do today out of identity protection rather than genuine desire?
(Hint: it’s most things you do)

```

**9:00pm**
```

When did I feel most alive today?
When did I feel most dead?

```

---

### Additional Contemplation Prompts
(Use during low-stimulation moments)

```

What would change if I stopped needing people to see me as [identity from Part 1, Question 9]?

Where in my life am I trading aliveness for safety?

What’s the smallest version of the person I want to become that I could be tomorrow?
```
