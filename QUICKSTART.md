# Quick Start Guide

Complete Dan Koe's "Fix Your Entire Life in 1 Day" protocol in one focused session.

## ⏰ Time Required
- **Minimum**: 6-8 hours (core protocol)
- **Recommended**: Full day (8-12 hours for deep work)

## 📅 Preparation (Do This First)

### 1. Schedule Your Day
- [ ] Pick a specific date
- [ ] Block entire day on calendar
- [ ] Clear all commitments
- [ ] Inform others you're unavailable

### 2. Prepare Your Space
- [ ] Clean, quiet environment
- [ ] Comfortable seating
- [ ] Good lighting
- [ ] Temperature control
- [ ] Water and light snacks available

### 3. Eliminate Distractions
- [ ] Phone on airplane mode (or turned off)
- [ ] Computer in focus mode
- [ ] Social media logged out
- [ ] Email closed
- [ ] Door closed/do not disturb sign

### 4. Gather Materials
- [ ] Notebook or digital note-taking app
- [ ] Pen/pencil (if using paper)
- [ ] Access to worksheets (print or digital)
- [ ] Timer for breaks
- [ ] Optional: music for focus

## 🎯 The Protocol

### Morning (8:00 AM - 11:00 AM)

**8:00 - 8:30**: Settle in, review protocol, prepare mindset
- Read the [main README](README.md)
- Understand what you're about to do
- Set intention for the day

**8:30 - 9:15**: [Anti-Vision Exercise](worksheets/01-anti-vision.md)
- Face your nightmare scenario
- Write with brutal honesty
- Feel the emotions

**9:15 - 10:00**: [Vision Exercise](worksheets/02-vision.md)
- Define your ideal future
- Write in vivid detail
- Connect emotionally

**10:00 - 10:15**: BREAK (walk, stretch, hydrate)

**10:15 - 11:00**: [Identity Statement](worksheets/03-identity.md)
- Define who you must become
- Claim your new identity
- Set clear standards

---

### Afternoon (11:00 AM - 3:00 PM)

**11:00 - 11:30**: Light lunch + rest
- Healthy, light meal
- Brief walk if possible
- Stay energized

**11:30 - 12:30**: [Excavation Exercise](worksheets/04-excavation.md)
- Uncover hidden patterns
- Face uncomfortable truths
- Identify root causes

**12:30 - 12:45**: BREAK

**12:45 - 1:45**: [Constraints & Sacrifices](worksheets/05-constraints.md)
- Identify what must go
- Make hard decisions
- Commit to changes

**1:45 - 2:00**: BREAK (walk, fresh air)

**2:00 - 3:00**: [Goals & Action Plans](worksheets/06-goals.md)
- Set 10-year vision
- Define 1-year goals
- Plan 90-day focus
- Identify daily actions

---

### Evening (3:00 PM - 7:00 PM)

**3:00 - 3:30**: Light snack + energizer break
- Recharge for final push
- Brief walk or movement

**3:30 - 4:45**: [Gamification Strategy](worksheets/07-gamification.md)
- Design your life game
- Create reward systems
- Make it fun and engaging

**4:45 - 5:00**: BREAK

**5:00 - 6:30**: [Behavioral Systems](worksheets/08-systems.md)
- Design daily routines
- Build weekly systems
- Set up environment
- Create accountability

**6:30 - 7:00**: Final Review & Integration
- Review all worksheets
- Identify patterns
- Clarify next actions
- Make commitments

---

### Post-Protocol (7:00 PM onwards)

**7:00 - 7:30**: Celebrate & Relax
- Acknowledge what you accomplished
- Light dinner
- Decompress

**7:30 - 8:00**: Immediate Implementation
- **Take your first action RIGHT NOW**
- Set up tracking systems
- Schedule tomorrow's routine
- Make your first commitment real

**Before bed**:
- Prepare tomorrow's environment
- Set intentions for first day as new identity
- Review your identity statement
- Get quality sleep

## 🚫 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Rushing through exercises** - Take your time, go deep
2. **Being vague** - Specificity creates clarity
3. **Intellectualizing** - Feel the emotions, don't just think
4. **Not taking breaks** - Mental fatigue reduces quality
5. **Skipping worksheets** - Each builds on previous ones
6. **Not implementing** - Worksheets alone change nothing
7. **Waiting to start** - Begin the very next day
8. **Perfectionism** - Done is better than perfect
9. **Sharing too early** - Keep it private until you have momentum
10. **Giving up after setbacks** - Expect resistance, persist anyway

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**"I don't have a full day available"**
- Break it into 2-3 shorter sessions over a weekend
- Quality matters more than speed
- Minimum 3-4 hours per session

**"I'm stuck on a worksheet"**
- Move to next one and come back
- Write anything, even if imperfect
- Talk it out loud to yourself
- Take a walk and return with fresh perspective

**"This feels overwhelming"**
- That's normal - you're facing your entire life
- Take breaks when needed
- Remember: this creates clarity, not more confusion
- One step at a time

**"I'm not being honest with myself"**
- These are for you alone
- No one else will see them
- The cost of dishonesty is staying stuck
- Start with small truths and build up
