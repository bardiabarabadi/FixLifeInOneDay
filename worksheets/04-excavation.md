# Excavation Exercise

## Purpose
Uncover the hidden motivations, fears, and patterns driving your current behavior. Surface-level awareness isn't enough—you must dig deep.

## Instructions
Be brutally honest. This is for you alone. The goal is self-awareness, not self-judgment.

---

## Current Reality Assessment

### Habits & Patterns

**What do I actually do each day?**
(Not what you think you do, but what you really do)


**What results have these habits produced?**


**What patterns keep repeating in my life?**


**What do I say I value vs. what my actions show I value?**
