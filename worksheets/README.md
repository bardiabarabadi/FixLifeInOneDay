# Worksheets Directory

This directory contains the practical templates for completing Dan Koe's "Fix Your Entire Life in 1 Day" protocol.

## 📋 Complete the Worksheets in Order

### Morning Session (2-3 hours)
1. **[01-anti-vision.md](01-anti-vision.md)** - Define your nightmare scenario
2. **[02-vision.md](02-vision.md)** - Define your ideal future
3. **[03-identity.md](03-identity.md)** - Clarify who you must become

### Afternoon Session (2-3 hours)
4. **[04-excavation.md](04-excavation.md)** - Uncover hidden patterns and motivations
5. **[05-constraints.md](05-constraints.md)** - Identify what must be eliminated

### Evening Session (2-3 hours)
6. **[06-goals.md](06-goals.md)** - Set concrete objectives and action plans
7. **[07-gamification.md](07-gamification.md)** - Design your life transformation game
8. **[08-systems.md](08-systems.md)** - Build behavioral systems for success

## 💡 How to Use These Worksheets

### Option 1: Digital Completion
1. Copy each worksheet to your personal notes app or create a private fork
2. Fill them out directly in markdown format
3. Keep them private and accessible for regular review

### Option 2: Print and Write
1. Print out each worksheet
2. Complete by hand for deeper processing
3. Store in a dedicated folder or binder
4. Review regularly

### Option 3: Hybrid Approach
1. Read the digital version
2. Use a notebook or journal to answer
3. Organize responses by worksheet sections
4. Digitize key insights later

## ✅ Tips for Success

1. **Block Dedicated Time**: Reserve a full day without interruptions
2. **Be Brutally Honest**: These are for you alone—authenticity is essential
3. **Write Freely**: Don't overthink—get thoughts out first, refine later
4. **Feel the Emotions**: Especially for anti-vision and vision exercises
5. **Be Specific**: Vague answers produce vague results
6. **Review Regularly**: Revisit these weekly/monthly to stay aligned
7. **Update as Needed**: Your answers will evolve—that's growth

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- **Privacy**: Keep these worksheets private. Raw honesty requires safety.
- **No Judgment**: This is self-discovery, not self-criticism.
- **Complete All**: Skipping worksheets weakens the entire system.
- **Take Breaks**: Mental fatigue is real—step away when needed.
- **Action Required**: Worksheets alone change nothing—implementation is everything.

## 🔄 After Completion

Once you've completed all worksheets:

1. Review your complete protocol
2. Identify your immediate next actions
3. Take the first action TODAY
4. Set up your tracking systems
5. Schedule your first weekly review
6. Begin living as your new identity
