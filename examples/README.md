# Example Worksheet Completions

This directory contains **anonymized examples** of completed worksheets to help you understand the depth and honesty required.

## Important Notes

⚠️ **These are examples only** - Your answers should reflect YOUR truth, not match these examples.

⚠️ **All examples are anonymized** - Personal details have been changed to protect privacy.

⚠️ **Depth matters** - Notice the specificity and emotional honesty in these examples.

## How to Use These Examples

1. **Read them AFTER trying yourself first**
   - Don't let examples constrain your thinking
   - Your truth is unique to you

2. **Notice the patterns**
   - Specificity over vagueness
   - Emotional connection
   - Honest self-assessment
   - Clear cause-and-effect

3. **Use as inspiration, not template**
   - Your life is different
   - Your goals are different
   - Your challenges are different
   - Your answers should be too

4. **Learn the level of depth**
   - Surface-level answers won't create change
   - Go deeper than comfortable
   - Be more honest than usual
   - Face uncomfortable truths

## Contributing Examples

If you've completed the protocol and want to help others:

1. **Anonymize completely**
   - Remove all personally identifying details
   - Change specifics while keeping essence
   - Protect your privacy

2. **Focus on quality**
   - Show depth of reflection
   - Demonstrate honesty
   - Illustrate insights gained
   - Keep it real

3. **Submit via pull request**
   - Follow [contributing guidelines](../CONTRIBUTING.md)
   - Include context about what made it effective
   - Explain insights gained from the exercise
